# ml-sales

Examples of Machine Learning for Sales

Includes:

1. Random Forest (supervised)
   
A random forest analysis for sales uses a machine learning method that combines many decision trees to predict future sales numbers and find out which factors drive customer purchases.
Applications: Sales Forecasting: Predicting next week's or next month's revenue based on past trends, holidays, and promotions. Product Performance: Identifying why certain items become bestsellers or why ratings influence customer demand. Marketing Mix: Measuring how different ad channels impact actual sales growth.


3. K-Means Clustering (unsupervised)
   
K-Means clustering is an unsupervised machine learning method that groups unlabeled sales or customer data into K distinct clusters based on feature similarities.
Applications: Customer Segmentation: Identify high-value buyers, frequent shoppers, or at-risk churn groups to target with tailored marketing.Inventory & Regional Planning: Group store performance or product sales patterns to optimize stock distribution.Discount Strategies: Find shopper groups that respond best to promotional markdowns.

5. Q-Learning (reinforcement learning)
   
Helps sales teams optimize decision-making, dynamic pricing, and customer engagement by learning the best actions through trial and error.
Applications: Dynamic Pricing: Automatically raising or lowering prices based on real-time demand and inventory levels to maximize revenue. Personalized Discounts: Deciding which customer gets a special offer and how large that discount should be to close a sale without hurting profit margins. Marketing & Sales Funnel Optimization: Guiding a customer smoothly from their first website visit to a final purchase by picking the right engagement step at each stage.

7. Connecting to SQLite (SQL) and plotting with matplotlib.
